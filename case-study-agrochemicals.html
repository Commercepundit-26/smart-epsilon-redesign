THE CHALLENGE

Blind past the warehouse door.

Operating across 100+ countries, this agrochemical manufacturer had already built a world-class production and distribution network. But the moment product left the factory, visibility started to fade. Leadership wanted more than operational tidiness. They wanted a straight answer to one question:

"Where is every product, and what’s happening to it across the entire channel?"

Without that answer, the business couldn't see secondary sales in real time, forecast demand accurately, or make fast commercial calls with confidence.

What We Deployed

An unbroken thread from Identity to Loyalty.

Every product was given a unique digital identity at the point of manufacturing. From there, six connected capabilities carried that identity through the entire commercial lifecycle.

Identity Management

A unique digital identity assigned to every product at the point of manufacturing

Plant Operations

Accurate, authenticated data capture at every step of production

Supply Chain Traceability

Every movement tracked across warehouses, distributors, and retailers

Sales Performance

Verified channel activity converted into measurable commercial insight

Consumer Engagement

Visibility extended past retail, connecting products directly to end users

Loyalty Management

Consumer interactions turned into long-term engagement, continuously enriching product intelligence
